You're a lot more likely to succeed learning anything if **you're part of a group**. Coding is no different.

If you'd like to get help, chat about your coding journey, or just hang out with others, there are some places to do it:
- [Our forum](https://jiki.io/r/forum): The place to go for slower, long-form conversation. **Go deeper** here.
- [Exercism's Discord](https://jiki.io/r/discord): The place to go for **real-time chat** with others who are online. Look for the #jiki-chat and #jiki-get-help channels.
- [YouTube](https://jiki.io/r/youtube): Join our AMAs (Ask Me Anything) and other **livestreams** to ask me your questions!

Remember that there are people from lots of different cultures in these spaces, so **please be respectful** to everyone as you chat.

See you there!
