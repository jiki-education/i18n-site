Nice work! Reading from arrays is handy, but being able to build them up piece by piece is where they really come into their own. Loop over something, collect the bits you care about into a new array, and hand it back — that's a pattern you'll reach for again and again.

Next up: dictionaries. These let you store values against named keys rather than just positions. Have fun!
