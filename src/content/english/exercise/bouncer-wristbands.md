You're back working as a bouncer, but this time for a local community event where the venue has a wristband system that give access to different areas based on age.

You have a <define>`getAge()`</define> function that returns the age of the person who you need to give a wristband to. You need to determine what category they fall into, then use one of four functions to give them the right wristband:

- Children are under 13
- Teens are 13-17 inclusive
- Adults are 18-64
- Seniors are 65+

The four functions are <define>`giveChildWristband()`</define>, <define>`giveTeenWristband()`</define>, <define>`giveAdultWristband()`</define> and <define>`giveSeniorWristband()`</define>.

Each scenario has a different aged person. Get their age and give them the correct wristband!

Have fun!
